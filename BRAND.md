# 🎨 QuickEats — Brand Identity & Design System Specification

## 1. Brand Philosophy & Overview
**QuickEats** is an AI-powered, real-time culinary logistics platform. The brand identity reflects **craftsmanship, intelligence, speed, and visual elegance**, moving away from standard, generic food delivery app clones.

---

## 2. Voice & Tone Guidelines
- **Confident & Helpful**: QuickEats communicates with precision and warmth. Avoid technical jargon when talking to customers.
- **Friendly & Efficient**: Short, crisp copy that respects the user's time.
- **Micro-Copy Example**:
  - *Standard Clone*: "Something went wrong. Error 500."
  - *QuickEats Voice*: "Our kitchen ran into a brief hiccup fetching that dish. Let's try again!"

---

---

## 4. Typography Pairing
- **Display & Headings**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Weights: 700 Bold, 800 ExtraBold) — Modern, rounded, geometric, and high-impact.
- **Body & Interface**: `Inter` (Weights: 400 Regular, 500 Medium, 600 SemiBold) — Ultra-legible for menus, prices, and status indicators.

---

## 5. Logo & SVG Assets
- **Wordmark**: `CraveCraft` with an integrated emerald leaf/lightning sparkle badge.
